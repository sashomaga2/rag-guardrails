# Home Office Equipment

## Budget
Each employee has a home office equipment budget of 400 EUR every 2 years.
The budget covers monitors, keyboards, mice, headsets and docking stations.
Office chairs and desks are covered only with a recommendation from occupational health.

## Claiming expenses
Submit receipts in Concur within 30 days of purchase.
Purchases above the budget need written approval from your manager before you buy.
Equipment bought with the budget belongs to Northwind and must be returned when you leave the company.
