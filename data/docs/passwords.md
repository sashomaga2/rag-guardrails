# Password Policy

## Requirements
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long.
Passphrases made of several random words are recommended over complex short passwords.
Passwords are not rotated on a schedule; you must change your password only if it may have been compromised.

## Password manager
Northwind provides 1Password Business to every employee for storing work credentials.
Never store work passwords in browser password managers or in plain text files.
Never share your password with anyone, including the IT helpdesk; IT staff will never ask for it.

## Account lockout and reset
Your account is locked for 15 minutes after 10 failed login attempts.
You can reset a forgotten password yourself at the self-service portal id.northwind.example.
Password resets require verification with Microsoft Authenticator.
