# Laptop Refresh Program

## Refresh cycle
Standard employees receive a new laptop every 4 years.
Engineering staff receive a new laptop every 3 years because of heavier workloads.
Laptops that fail outside the warranty period can be replaced early after an IT assessment.

## How to request a refresh
Request a refresh through the ServiceNow catalog item called "Hardware Refresh".
Your manager must approve the request before IT orders the device.
Delivery usually takes 10 business days in Bulgaria and 15 business days in other EU countries.

## Returning the old device
Return your old laptop to IT within 14 days after you receive the new one.
IT wipes returned devices with a certified data erasure process before reuse or recycling.
Back up local files to OneDrive before you return the old laptop.
