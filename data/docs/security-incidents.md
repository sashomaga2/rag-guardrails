# Security Incident Reporting

## What to report
Report suspected phishing, malware, lost devices and accidental data sharing as security incidents.
Use the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook for suspicious emails instead of forwarding them.

## Reporting deadline and contact
Report every security incident to security@northwind.example within 1 hour of noticing it.
Outside business hours call the security on-call line at extension 7777.

## If your laptop may be compromised
Disconnect the laptop from the network but do not power it off, because memory evidence may be needed.
Do not try to remove malware yourself.
Lost or stolen laptops must also be reported to your manager on the same day.
