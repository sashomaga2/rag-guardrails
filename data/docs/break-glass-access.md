# Break-glass admin access

## When to use it

Break-glass access is the emergency administrator account for the identity platform. Use it only
when single sign-on is down for everyone and the normal admin accounts cannot log in.

## How to get it

The IT duty manager approves every break-glass session by phone. Open a P1 incident ticket first and
put the ticket number in the approval call. The duty manager releases the account from the
privileged access vault for 4 hours.

## After the incident

The break-glass account is disabled automatically after 4 hours. Rotate its credentials in the vault
and attach the session recording to the incident ticket within one business day.
